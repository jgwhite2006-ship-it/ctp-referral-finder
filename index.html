Priority: High children's hospitals and pediatric practices, the most common referral source in national research. Medium family clinics, general hospitals, behavioral health, and schools, which often refer indirectly. Low childcare and competitor therapy clinics, shown for awareness.

Pediatric specialty (unverified): this column shows when a provider at that address, or with a matching last name, lists a pediatric specialty in the federal NPI registry. Providers choose their own specialty there and no one checks it, and the address match can occasionally link the wrong doctor. It does not mean the doctor is board certified. To confirm board certification, look the doctor up by name on the American Board of Pediatrics site ("Verify Certification") or on Certification Matters. Both are free.
